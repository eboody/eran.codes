Three things you should know about NetBird

It's simple and secure

Creating a NetBird network takes under 5 minutes, providing encrypted connections without complex firewall setups. Its Zero Trust model ensures only authorized users and devices can access internal resources.

It connects anything anywhere

NetBird works on Linux, Windows, macOS, mobile devices, Docker containers, and even routers. It’s infrastructure-agnostic, allowing seamless connectivity between resources across different clouds and on-premises.

It's open source

While you can always rely on NetBird Cloud, the platform is distributed under a permissive BSD-3 license and can be self-hosted on your servers, allowing users to review the code and run it on their own infrastructure.
